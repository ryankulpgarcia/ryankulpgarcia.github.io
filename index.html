What We Do

Investigation & Security Services

From personal matters to complex corporate cases — and from threat assessment to executive protection — Prama Intel handles every engagement with precision and discretion.

Surveillance

Stationary and mobile surveillance using industry-standard equipment. Video and photographic documentation for personal, legal, or insurance matters.

Background Checks

Comprehensive background investigations on individuals — employment history, criminal records, civil history, assets, and more.

Infidelity Investigations

Discreet, compassionate investigations for suspected spousal or partner infidelity. All findings documented with care and confidentiality.

Corporate Investigations

Employee misconduct, intellectual property theft, competitor intelligence, vendor fraud, and workplace investigation services for businesses.

Legal Support & Skip Tracing

Process service, witness location, missing persons, and asset location for attorneys, law firms, and individuals pursuing legal action.

Insurance Fraud

Claimant surveillance and investigation for insurance companies and attorneys to detect fraudulent workers' comp, disability, and liability claims.

Child Custody Investigations

Documentation of parental fitness, lifestyle observations, and evidence gathering for family court proceedings. Sensitive and professional handling.

Digital & OSINT Investigations

Open-source intelligence gathering, social media investigations, and digital footprint analysis for online harassment, fraud, or identity verification.
