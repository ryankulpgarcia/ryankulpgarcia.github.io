Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire a private investigator? +
Cost varies based on type of investigation, time, tools, and resources required. We offer free initial consultations to assess your case and provide a clear estimate before any commitment is made.
Is everything kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All inquiries, case details, and findings are held in strict confidence. We operate with attorney-client level discretion and will never share your information without your consent.
Are you licensed and insured in Ohio? +
Yes. Prama Intel is fully licensed and insured under Ohio law as a private investigation and security services firm. You can verify our license through the Ohio Department of Homeland Security PISGS system.
Can evidence gathered be used in court? +
Yes. We conduct all investigations using lawful methods and document everything to meet court-admissible standards. Our reports are designed to hold up in civil and criminal proceedings.
What areas do you serve? +
We are based in Cleveland and serve all of Northeast Ohio, including Akron, Canton, Youngstown, and surrounding areas. We are also available for cases nationwide when needed.
How quickly can you start a case? +
We respond to urgent cases immediately. Most investigations and security engagements can begin the same day or the next day after consultation and approval.
Do you offer armed security services? +
Yes. We provide both armed and unarmed security personnel for executive protection, event security, personal escorts, and property security. Personnel assignments are based on your threat profile and needs.
Who typically hires a private investigator? +
Our clients include private individuals, businesses, law firms, insurance companies, and corporations. Anyone who needs verified facts, documented evidence, or professional security can benefit from our services.
